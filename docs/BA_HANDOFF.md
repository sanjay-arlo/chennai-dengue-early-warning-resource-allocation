# BA Handoff — Chennai Dengue Early Warning

## Objective
Prioritise wards for vector-control, screening and field response using case pressure, rolling baseline, breeding-source removal and screening coverage.

## Stakeholders
Public-health leadership; ward health teams; vector-control teams; laboratories; field supervisors; programme/finance teams.

## Core requirements
- Compare weekly cases with the four-week baseline.
- Assign transparent risk tiers.
- Identify resource gaps by ward.
- Rank interventions by explainable drivers.
- Keep portfolio data clearly labelled as synthetic.

## Decision rule
Prioritise wards with elevated trend ratio and absolute cases, then adjust for incomplete field activity. Risk tier supports review and resource planning; it is not a diagnosis.

## Acceptance criteria
Dashboard loads client-side; trend and risk KPIs reconcile; filters work; top wards are explainable; synthetic-data disclaimer is visible.

## Production controls
Verified surveillance and lab data, ward master, privacy controls, alert governance, epidemiologist sign-off, QA and audit trail.
